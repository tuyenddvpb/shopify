90-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to love your VitalRay. If you're not satisfied for any reason, you can request a refund within 90 days of the delivery date.

How to start a return

  1. Email us at support@vitalray.shop with your order number and the reason for your return.
  2. Our support team will reply within 1 business day with your return instructions and return address. Please do not send items back without contacting us first — returns sent to the wrong address cannot be processed.
  3. Pack the product with all included accessories and ship it back using a tracked shipping service.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may need extra time to post the refund.

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

Please inspect your order when it arrives. If the item is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, email support@vitalray.shop within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo or short video of the issue. We'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund — no return shipping cost to you.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order for a full refund before it ships. Contact us as soon as possible — orders usually leave our fulfillment center within 1–3 business days.

One guarantee per customer

The 90-day money-back guarantee applies to one unit per customer on the first order.

Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to, and does not affect, your rights under the consumer laws of your country, including the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, the Australian Consumer Law, and applicable laws in the United States and Canada.

Questions?
