Free tracked shipping

We offer free tracked shipping on all orders to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays). You'll receive a confirmation email when your order is placed, and a shipping confirmation with your tracking number once it ships.

Delivery times are estimates and start from the day your order ships. Peak seasons, customs checks or carrier delays can occasionally add a few days.

Where we ship from

Your order is shipped directly to you from our fulfillment partners' warehouses. If you order more than one item, they may arrive in separate packages.

Tracking your order

Use the tracking number in your shipping confirmation email, or visit our Track Your Order page. Tracking can take 2–3 business days to show updates after the label is created.

Customs, duties and taxes

Depending on your country, import duties or taxes may apply to your order. Where they are not collected at checkout, they are the responsibility of the customer. If you have any questions about this before ordering, please contact us.

Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking hasn't updated for 10 business days, or your order hasn't arrived within 25 business days of shipping, contact us. We'll work with the carrier and, if the package is lost, send a free replacement or a full refund.

Wrong address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. Contact us within 12 hours of ordering if you need to change it. We can't be responsible for orders shipped to an incorrect address provided at checkout.
