30-day returns

We want you to be happy with your order. If something isn't right, you can request a return within 30 days of the delivery date.

To be eligible, the item must be unused, in the same condition you received it, and in its original packaging with all accessories.

How to start a return

  1. Email us at support@nestlyday.com with your order number, the item you'd like to return and the reason.
  2. We'll reply within 1 business day with your return instructions and return address. Please do not send items back without contacting us first — returns sent to the wrong address cannot be processed.
  3. Pack the item securely and ship it back using a tracked shipping service.

Items we can't accept for return

  • Opened or used beauty, skincare and personal-care products, for hygiene reasons (unless they arrived damaged or defective)
  • Underwear, socks and earrings, for hygiene reasons
  • Gift cards
  • Items marked as final sale at the time of purchase

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may need extra time to post the refund.

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect. Original shipping fees are not refundable.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

Please inspect your order when it arrives. If an item is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, email support@nestlyday.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo or short video of the issue. We'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund — no return shipping cost to you.

Exchanges

The fastest way to get a different size or color is to return the item you have and place a new order for the one you want.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order for a full refund before it ships. Contact us as soon as possible — orders usually leave our fulfillment partners within 1–3 business days.

VitalRay Wrist: 90-day money-back guarantee

The VitalRay Wrist comes with an extended 90-day money-back guarantee. You can request a refund within 90 days of delivery, following the steps above. This extended guarantee applies to one unit per customer on the first order.

Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to, and does not affect, your rights under the consumer laws of your country, including the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, the Australian Consumer Law, and applicable laws in the United States and Canada.
